Clerid for Enterprise & Government

Audit-ready evidence pulled from the records you already have, without adding headcount to produce it by hand.

What it does for your program

Risk like this, vendor concentration, capital projects buried in budget documents, contract actions buried in meeting minutes, is currently surfaced, if at all, through manual review: slow, inconsistent, and hard to defend under audit. Clerid replaces that with a repeatable pipeline: pull in a source document, extract the structured records inside it, classify what it finds, and flag what needs a person to look at it, with a sourced trail back to the original document every time. What used to be an ad hoc attestation becomes a standing, reviewable record.

How it deploys

Clerid runs entirely on infrastructure Panderose owns and operates, built for Controlled Unclassified Information environments from the start. Source data your program provides is never processed by external services. The output slots into your existing workflow alongside what you already have.

Where it stands today

Clerid's vendor economic dependency screening is currently proposed for the Defense Logistics Agency under a DoW SBIR Phase I effort, with the same engine already validated against live SAM.gov, USASpending.gov, and SEC EDGAR data and running two other configurations: capital projects extraction and contract action tracking. We're in active conversations with DLA now, and expect to be demonstration-ready within the year. If your program has a similar records intelligence need, we'd like to talk.
