Clerid

Audit-ready evidence on which of your vendors are financially dependent on your contracts, without adding headcount to produce it by hand.

What it does for your program

Vendor concentration risk is currently identified, if at all, through manual review: slow, inconsistent, and hard to defend under audit. Clerid replaces that with a repeatable pipeline: it resolves each vendor's identity, traces their contract spend, locates their public financial filings, computes a dependency ratio against defined thresholds, and produces an evidence package your auditors can verify independently. What used to be an ad hoc attestation becomes a standing, reviewable record.

How it deploys

Clerid runs on infrastructure Panderose owns and operates, not third-party cloud, built for Controlled Unclassified Information environments from the start. Source data your program provides is never processed by external services. The output is designed to sit alongside your existing financial reporting workflow, not replace it.

Where it stands today

Clerid is currently proposed for the Defense Logistics Agency under a DoW SBIR Phase I effort, with a working prototype already validated against live SAM.gov, USASpending.gov, and SEC EDGAR data. We're in active conversations with DLA now, and expect to be demonstration-ready within the year. If your program has a similar vendor-dependency or supplier-concentration question, we'd like to talk.
