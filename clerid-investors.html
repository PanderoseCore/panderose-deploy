Clerid for Investors

A federal financial-compliance gap, a working prototype validated against live government and public-market data, and a funding path that starts with non-dilutive federal capital.

The opportunity

DLA alone obligated $55.4 billion across 8,500+ suppliers in FY2025 with no systematic way to measure vendor concentration risk. The same gap exists, in some form, across every one of the 24 CFO Act agencies, each with its own supplier base, its own audit exposure, and the same missing capability. DoD has posted eight consecutive years of disclaimer of opinion on its financial statements, with 26 material weaknesses identified in FY2025 alone. Vendor economic dependency is a well-defined slice of that problem, not a hypothetical one.

Traction

Clerid is not a concept. It's a working prototype (TRL 5), validated end-to-end against live external systems: SAM.gov, USASpending.gov, and SEC EDGAR. Tested against a real 32-vendor set, it successfully measured 28, with dependency ratios ranging from near-zero up to 9.37%. The unresolved cases were typed exceptions: a wrong-entity refusal, a privately-held vendor, two foreign entities without comparable filings, which is itself a useful signal about where the method's boundaries are.

Funding path

Clerid is proposed for a 12-month DoW SBIR Phase I effort, targeting TRL 6: a validated golden dataset, an audit-defensible evidence pipeline, and a demonstration-ready product by the end of Phase I. Phase II targets production transition at DLA plus evaluation at a second agency. Panderose is pursuing this entirely in-house, with no subcontractors and no FFRDCs, which keeps the cap table simple and the technical ownership concentrated with the founding team.

Path to market

The roadmap is sequential and deliberately narrow at first: prove the mechanism at DLA, extend to a second federal agency, then open a commercial track for private-sector compliance and internal-audit use cases that share the same underlying problem: knowing which counterparties you're financially entangled with.
