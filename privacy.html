Effective date: June 2026

What we collect
Contact information you submit through the site: name, email, and anything you write in a message field. The contact form opens a pre-filled email to us; we don't run it through a server or database of our own.

None. This site does not use cookies, analytics, or third-party tracking scripts of any kind.

To respond to inquiries and fulfill contracted services. Nothing you submit is used for marketing outreach beyond replying to what you sent us.
Third parties
We do not sell or share personal data. Information you provide is not shared outside Panderose except where required by law or a signed agreement.
Data retention
We keep inquiry correspondence as long as it's relevant to an active conversation or engagement, and delete or archive it when it's not.
Your rights
You can ask us what information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, at any time. Contact us and we'll respond.
Children's privacy
This site is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.
Changes to this policy
If this policy changes materially, we'll update the effective date above.
