About

Panderose is a software company built around one problem: the records that matter to institutions, financial, contractual, operational, are scattered across incompatible systems and too complex to review by hand. We build the tools that close that gap, starting with Clerid, our records intelligence engine. Founded in April 2026 in Prescott, Arizona.

Origin

Complexity in government and enterprise records has outpaced the systems designed to manage it. Vendor relationships shift, ownership structures overlap, and risk builds up in the space between disconnected data sources. Panderose started with the sharpest version of that problem, screening vendor economic dependency for the Defense Logistics Agency, and built an engine general enough to solve it wherever the same shape of problem shows up.

Mission

Panderose gives institutions, government and enterprise alike, visibility into risk before it becomes disruption. Every system we build is held to the standard defense and audit-grade work demands: verifiable, source-traceable, and built specifically for the problem at hand.

Future

Panderose is built to grow. The SBIR effort with DLA is only the first proof point, laid on an architecture designed to extend well past a single program or agency. From here, Panderose is positioned to move into aerospace, logistics, and any other domain defined by the same problem: hidden risk in complex, scattered records.
