Capabilities

Panderose builds four categories of software for defense and federal procurement programs. Each is purpose-built for government data and compliance requirements, not adapted from commercial tooling.

Federal Procurement Analytics

Government procurement and award data is large, multi-source, and inconsistently structured across agencies. Panderose builds analytics tooling that ingests award, obligation, and contractor data and surfaces it in a form usable by compliance and oversight teams.

  • Contract spend and obligation tracking across federal award data sources
  • Vendor and contractor risk signals derived from award history
  • Structured outputs designed for audit and oversight review, not just dashboards

Defense Supply Chain Software

Defense logistics programs depend on visibility into vendors and sub-tier suppliers that primes and program offices often lack. Panderose builds software that maps supplier relationships and flags disruption or concentration risk before it affects a program.

  • Vendor and sub-tier supplier mapping for defense logistics programs
  • Disruption and single-source risk flagging
  • Designed to integrate with existing program office data, not replace it

Compliance Automation

Federal audit and oversight programs require records that are structured, verifiable, and reproducible. Panderose builds automation that generates these records as a byproduct of normal system operation, with controls aligned to NIST SP 800-171 and CMMC Level 2 practices.

  • Automated generation of audit-ready, structured compliance records
  • Controls aligned to NIST SP 800-171 and CMMC Level 2 practices
  • Built for unclassified government workloads on infrastructure Panderose owns and operates

See our security and compliance posture.

Federal Data Systems

Government data is rarely clean or co-located. Panderose builds pipelines that normalize and reason over complex, multi-source federal data, so downstream analytics and compliance tooling can rely on a consistent structure.

  • Normalization pipelines for multi-source, multi-format government data
  • Reasoning layers built for procurement and oversight use cases
  • Foundation for the procurement analytics and compliance automation capabilities above
