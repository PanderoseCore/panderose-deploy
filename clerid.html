Clerid

Clerid turns unstructured records into structured, usable data, built first for federal vendor oversight and now extending into commercial industries.

The problem

The records that matter, filings, budget documents, meeting minutes, aren't missing. They're just unreadable at scale. No team has the hours to comb through hundreds of pages by hand looking for the one contract action, project, or risk signal that actually matters.

So it doesn't get read. Opportunities get missed and risk goes unflagged, and everyone finds out the hard way: after an audit, after a bid closes, after the moment to act has already passed.

Clerid's solution

Clerid is one engine that does the same job no matter what it's pointed at: pull in a source document, extract the structured records inside it, classify what it finds, and flag what needs a person to look at it, with a sourced trail back to the original document every time.

That engine already runs three ways: screening a vendor base for economic dependency risk, pulling capital projects out of unstructured budget documents, and tracking contract actions buried in meeting minutes. Adding a new use case is just a configuration change.
